Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Straightforward answers about our platform, Meta integration, and team seats.

What do I need to connect WhatsApp?
You need a Meta Business Account and an active WhatsApp Business Cloud API app. We guide you step-by-step through configuring your Phone Number ID and Webhook credentials.
Do account Owners and Admins count as seats?
Yes. In order to ensure fair capacity management, each team member with active login access (Owner, Administrator, or Staff) counts towards your plan's allocated seats.
How does AI knowledge retrieval work?
Your FAQs and service descriptions provide workspace-scoped retrieval context for the assistant. An administrator must configure and review your knowledge sources before enabling automated replies.
Can we test the assistant before connecting our live number?
Absolutely. Our onboarding wizard features an interactive synthetic simulator where you can test conversational replies in English, Malayalam, Manglish, or Hindi without sending live messages.
